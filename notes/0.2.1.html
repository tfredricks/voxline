Fix Sparkle auto-update on machines with a previous dev build **Bug fix:** Auto-update failed with "An error occurred while launching the installer" on machines that had a development build of voxline previously installed. The app sandbox was missing the required mach-lookup entitlements (`-spks` and `-spki`) for Sparkle's installer XPC service, which caused the connection to be denied when stale XPC authorization state from the dev build was present. **If your update button is stuck:** Delete voxline from Applications and reinstall from the latest DMG. Auto-updates will work normally after a fresh install.
