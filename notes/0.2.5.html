Highlight text in any app, press your dictation hotkey, and speak how to change it — **"make this a bullet list"**, **"make this cleaner"**, **"make this shorter"** — and voxline rewrites the selection in place. It's a normal ⌘Z-undoable edit, and the recording pill offers quick follow-up refinements just like it does after dictation. No text selected? The hotkey dictates exactly as before. Dictation and transforms now paste via ⌘V wherever possible instead of falling back to synthetic typing, so text lands reliably in apps like Notes that ignore synthetic keystrokes.
