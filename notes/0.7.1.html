### Changed - **Developer terms.** Cleanup now repairs misheard software terms ("dove" → "dev", "get hub" → "GitHub"), the speech engine gets a built-in list of developer vocabulary as recognition hints, and editors, terminals, Claude and Codex share one developer style that punctuates prose prompts and leaves commands as typed.
