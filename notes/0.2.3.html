fix(updates): enable SUEnableInstallerLauncherService for sandboxed auto-install Without this Info.plist key, Sparkle ran its installer launcher directly inside the sandboxed process and called SMJobSubmit from there. The sandbox blocked that mach call so the installer never launched, causing every auto-update installation to silently fail. With SUEnableInstallerLauncherService = YES, Sparkle uses the bundled Installer.xpc which runs outside the sandbox and can submit the launchd job correctly. The mach-lookup exceptions added in v0.2.1 remain needed for the sandboxed app to connect to the installer once it is running. Also bumps MARKETING_VERSION to 0.2.3. Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 4.6
